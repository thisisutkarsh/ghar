Ghar

A simple home screen for our family's Google TV, made so my parents can find something to watch without opening six apps.

What it does

Ghar replaces the TV's busy home screen with large, easy rows. It shows what the family was watching, the apps we subscribe to, news channels, and three movie rows built from TMDB data:

New Hindi moviesReleased in the last six months, on our streaming apps.
ClassicsWell-rated Hindi films from before 2000.
RecommendedFilms similar to what we watched recently.

How it uses the TMDB API

  • Lists Hindi films by release date, rating and language.
  • Shows each film's poster, rating, runtime, year and Indian age certificate, so it can skip adult-rated films.
  • Checks which streaming services offer a film in India, so only films on our subscriptions appear.
  • Finds films similar to the ones we watched.
  • Results are saved on the TV and refreshed about once a day, so requests stay low.

Who it is for

One TV in one home. Ghar is personal and non-commercial: no ads, no accounts, no data collected or shared, and it is not distributed to anyone else.
